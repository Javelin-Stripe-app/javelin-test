04 — Logo System

Logo

The Precision Mark — Concept A. An abstract geometric mark evoking a javelin's trajectory. Single diagonal stroke with arrowhead, oriented lower-left to upper-right.

The Precision Mark (Concept A)

An abstract geometric mark evoking a javelin's trajectory — a single diagonal line with arrowhead that suggests forward motion, precision, and directness. Maps directly to the brand essence: direct, capable, immediate.

  • → Single geometric form — not a literal javelin illustration
  • → Diagonal orientation (lower-left → upper-right) = forward/upward motion
  • → 2–3 geometric shapes maximum
  • → No gradients in the primary mark

06 — Voice & Tone

Voice & Tone

Four attributes. Each paired with a "this / not that" example. Inside the Stripe App UI, voice IS the brand — it's the primary lever Javelin controls.

Direct
Says what it means. Does what it says.
Say this
"Here's your MRR dashboard."
Not this
"We've prepared a comprehensive analysis of your recurring revenue metrics."
Capable
Quietly competent. Never needs to boast.
Say this
"Done. I updated 47 subscriptions and rolled back 2 that had conflicts."
Not this
"Our powerful AI engine leveraged cutting-edge technology to process your request."
Clear
Asks exactly what it needs to know.
Say this
"I need to know: are you looking at all customers or just active ones?"
Not this
"To optimize your experience, could you provide additional parameters?"
Honest
Transparent about limits. Never hides behind errors.
Say this
"I can't undo a confirmed payment. Want me to issue a refund instead?"
Not this
"An error has occurred in processing your request."

08 — Brand Usage

Brand Dos & Don'ts

Messaging, visual, and competitive positioning rules in one place.

Do

  • Lead with the user's problem, then your solution
  • Say "Javelin builds" — active, concrete
  • Use Stripe's own terminology (MRR, churn, subscriptions)
  • Be specific: "builds dashboards and automations"
  • Acknowledge limitations honestly
  • Show concrete examples ("Try: 'Show me churn by plan'")
  • Say "Stripe-native" to mean embedded in Dashboard
  • Position as builder (active, constructive)
  • Use high-contrast text on backgrounds
  • Keep the logo mark simple and geometric
  • Match Stripe's visual tone in marketplace images

Don't

  • Lead with features or technology
  • Say "Javelin empowers you to" — passive, vague
  • Invent new terminology for known concepts
  • Be abstract: "transforms your Stripe experience"
  • Overpromise capabilities at MVP stage
  • Describe capabilities abstractly without examples
  • Say "integrated with Stripe" (implies external tool)
  • Position as assistant or copilot — passive, advisory
  • Use light text on medium backgrounds
  • Use detailed illustrations in the logo
  • Show mockups with custom CSS or non-Stripe UI
09 — Brand Personality

Archetype

The Magician transforms complexity into tangible outcomes. The Sage grounds that power in trust and transparency — essential for an AI that modifies real business data.

Primary
The Magician

Transforms complex Stripe operations into effortless outcomes. "Describe what you want and it appears" is a fundamentally Magician promise.

Transforms complexity into simplicity
Makes the impossible feel achievable
Operates through understanding, not brute force
Delivers tangible outcomes, not just insights
Inspires confidence through competence
Supporting
The Sage

Tempers the Magician with trustworthiness. When an AI touches real business data, users need to trust what the system is doing.

Clear explanations of what was built and why
Data freshness indicators and audit trails
Honest about limitations — asks before guessing
Competent, measured tone over hype
Transparency earned through visibility

If Javelin were a person

"Javelin is the colleague who, when you describe a problem, says 'I can fix that' — and actually does, right then. Not the one who schedules a meeting to discuss it. Not the one who sends you a dashboard and says 'the data is all there.' The one who listens, understands, builds, and hands you the working solution with a brief explanation of what they did."

Positioning Statement · Internal

To founders and ops leads at small, growing companies who run their business on Stripe, Javelin is the AI-powered builder inside your Stripe Dashboard that turns plain-language instructions into working workflows, dashboards, and automations — because every other tool in the Stripe ecosystem is a read-only analyst. Javelin is the only one that builds.
