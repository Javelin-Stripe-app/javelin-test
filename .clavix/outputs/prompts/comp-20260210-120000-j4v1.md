# Improved Prompt

## Role
You are an experienced Product Manager partnering with me on early-stage product research for Javelin (withjavelin.com).

## Context
Javelin is an AI-powered builder that lets non-technical users customize Stripe — without code, directly inside the Stripe Dashboard via the Stripe Apps marketplace. Think "Shopify Sidekick, but for Stripe."

Critical framing: We are NOT Stripe. We are a third-party developer building a Stripe App. This means we operate within Stripe's platform constraints (App UI extensions, OAuth scopes, API rate limits, marketplace review process).

## Research Sources
1. **Shopify Magic/Sidekick** — Fetch https://www.shopify.com/magic and analyze: features, UX patterns, value proposition, how it's embedded in the platform, what makes it compelling
2. **`.ops/research/overview.md`** — Javelin's vision, target users, beachhead strategy, use cases, and GTM plan
3. **`.ops/research/market-research.md`** — Competitive landscape across Stripe, Shopify, and HubSpot ecosystems

## Deliverable
Generate a structured analysis report saved to `.ops/analysis/` covering:

1. **Shopify Sidekick Teardown** — What it does, how it works, key UX patterns, why merchants love it, what's transferable to Stripe
2. **Stripe Ecosystem Gap Analysis** — What Stripe users need that Stripe doesn't provide natively (drawn from research files + Sidekick patterns)
3. **Competitive Landscape** — Existing solutions in Stripe's ecosystem (Erdo AI, Hunchbank, Lemonado, Narrative BI, etc.), their strengths, weaknesses, and positioning gaps
4. **Platform Constraints & Opportunities** — What Stripe Apps can/can't do, Stripe Sigma overlap, risk of platform kill zone
5. **Javelin Differentiation** — How Javelin is positioned differently from both Stripe-native tools and third-party competitors (ecosystem-native, zero-setup, AI builder vs. AI analyst)
6. **Key Questions & Risks** — Open questions, assumptions to validate, risks to mitigate

## Constraints
- Be short and concise — no filler
- Do not make things up — only use information from research sources and the Shopify Magic page
- Think before answering — reason through each section
- If you have questions or find gaps, flag them explicitly in Section 6
- Do NOT write code or implement anything — this is research only
